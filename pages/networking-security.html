Networking & Security

Strong connections. Smarter protection. Peace of mind.

Service overview

A dependable network is the foundation of a productive business. Florigon Technology Consulting designs, deploys, and maintains secure, high-performance networks that keep your people, devices, and applications connected.

From professional Wi-Fi and wired infrastructure to firewalls, network segmentation, remote access, and UniFi networking solutions, we help protect your systems while keeping your business connected and productive.

How we can help

  • Wired and wireless networks
    Design and deploy wired networks and business Wi-Fi, including UniFi solutions.
  • Firewalls and network segmentation
    Set traffic rules and separate guest, staff, and device networks based on their access needs.
  • Secure remote access
    Give authorized staff access to the systems they need when working away from the office.
  • Proactive security assessments and infrastructure hardening
    Identify security gaps before they lead to incidents and strengthen configurations across networks, servers, and workstations.
